# An exact cost floor for reader 497 with two retained operands

**Scoped proof with exact finite corroboration. No new κ.** Retaining each of
reader 497's two earlier operands in its own descending owned cache cannot
improve its remaining root-772 stop in the PR6167 word. Even with free ordinary
setup, waiting, storage and erasure, the minimum recursive bill is

    2*1^t + 2*3^t + 5^t,  0<t<1,

attained by the original schedule. Every changed dimension recipe costs
strictly more. If the root meets the reader below its original six-dimensional
frame 520, the excess is at least `2*4^t-3^t-5^t > 0` per local call.

This answers one concrete continuation of Ali Romman's PR6167 and Matarener's
PR6214 handoff. The successful reader-451 retention rewrite does not transfer
directly: frame 737 contains the root's final direction, whereas reader 497's
first operand frame 519 does not. Retaining that earlier operand requires
paid transport; it cannot simply be made available at the new meeting frame.

The new [proof](PROOF.md) first reduces arbitrary finite monotone paths and
arbitrary common frames to their contact dimensions. It then proves a general
minimum for endpoint dimensions `(s,u,u+1,1,u+1)`, for every
`1<=s<=u-1`, by moving the second delivery to its original frame and applying
subadditivity and concavity. The source-specific integer certificate gives
the quantitative gap and independently corroborates that proof. Here the
supplier's tariff is `H_r=r^t`, with `t=1-C` in its recursive moment notation.

## Precisely what is covered

The reader ascends from frame 3 to 914, the loaded owned root descends from
529 to 595, and the original V, B and -4Q additions are preserved. Each earlier
operand may have one separate owned copy captured at its original cut/frame,
transported through any descending chain, and erased after use. All reader,
root and cache MOVEs are charged. Additional intermediate frames need not
belong to the original list. All productive targets and other work are fixed.

Mixed source representations, root cloning, public ascending carriers, changed
source prefixes or consumer cuts, new productive targets, and coordinated
changes to other paid work remain outside the result. The optimistic relaxation
grants free ordinary resources only to make rejection stronger; it is not a
claim that a physical cache has zero cost. The symbolic templates explicitly
record the copies, lifetime endpoints and erasure. No rounded implementation,
native phase replay, full caller admission or selected-frontier update follows.
