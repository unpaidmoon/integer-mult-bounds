# Selected-pool native floor at q2 event 58

The target is the six-bank suffix at the literal `query5 source J` entrance.
Prefix native mass is 54 and the exhibited suffix has native mass 22, so the
whole word has mass 76 against stock 72. A suffix of native mass 16 would put
the whole word at 70, under stock. Under the gate set below, every such suffix
has native mass at least 20, so 16 is impossible. A replay of the predecessor's
neutral rewrite shows that 22 is still achievable. No new κ.

## Gate set

Paid natives are only the selected pool operators and their inverses.

| Native | Polar rank | Address effect | Fresh-plane offset |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `K` and `K⁻¹` | 2 | none (translation by 3) | none |
| `T` and `T⁻¹` | 2 | none | bits 10 and 11, offset 3072 |
| `W` and `W⁻¹` | 4 | swap of planes 0 and 1 | none |

`T` commutes with `K` and with `W`. `T² = K²` is a central sign `s ≠ 1`, and
`T⁴ = 1`. ADD, rational SCALE, and BANK_SWAP are free of native rank. Extra
scratch banks are allowed and do not lower the count. A mixed product such as
`KW` is not a selected native. Rank-0 address gates are not in this gate set;
the predecessor's neutral rewrite already eliminates the two fresh-plane swaps
and the final sign, and the mass-22 replay below does not use them.

## Even cores

The pool closes as a group of 256 monomials. The subgroup `H = ⟨K, W⟩` is the
128 even monomials. Every pool monomial is either even or uniquely `T` times an
even monomial. A bank value is therefore `E + T C` with even rows `E, C`.

Left multiplication acts as follows.

- By `K` or `W` (or an inverse): both `E` and `C` are left-multiplied. Inverses
  stay in the even algebra because `K⁻¹ = s K` and `W⁻¹` is a word in `W`.
- By `T`: the new odd core is the old even row, and the new even row is `s`
  times the old core. `T⁻¹ = s T` does the same with one extra sign.
- ADD, SCALE, and BANK_SWAP: `ℚ`-linear combinations and permutations of rows.

Let `A = ℚ[H]`. After `t` applications of `T` or `T⁻¹`, every odd core lies in
a left `A`-module generated by `t` vectors in `A⁶`. The other gates preserve
any such module: `K` and `W` act by elements of `A`, and a rational combination
of module elements stays in the module. Each fresh query adds the current even
row of one bank, which is one new generator.

The output matrix of the neutral rewrite is exactly `T` applied on the left to
six even cores `(z₀, …, z₅)` in the inputs `(a, u, b, r, z, c)`:

```text
z0 = K a
z1 = b - r - W(K a + b + K z)
z2 = -K a + b - r - K z
z3 = K(u + b - W(K a + b + K z))
z4 = K z
z5 = c
```

So the output cores are these six rows, and they must lie in some
`t`-generator module.

## Six fresh queries

Let `ρ` be the permutation-and-phase representation of an even monomial on the
four low bits (dimension 16). The verifier checks that `ρ` is a representation
of all 128 even monomials. Placing the six cores into a 96×96 matrix
over `ℚ(i)` gives rank 96. Replacing the second core by a copy of the first
drops the rank to 80.

Five generators would write each core as `z_i = Σ_{p=1}^{5} a_{ip} v_p` with
`a_{ip} ∈ A`. Applying `ρ` blockwise would factor the 96×96 matrix through a
width of `5 · 16 = 80`, so its rank would be at most 80. Rank 96 forbids that.
Therefore `t ≥ 6`. Six fresh queries cost `6 · 2 = 12`.

This is a count of module generators, not of rational dimension. Left
multiplication by `K` or `W` can enlarge the rational span of the odd parts
inside a single cyclic module, so a dimension count of six does not by itself
force six fresh queries. The generator count does.

## Two `K` queries

Let `B = ℚ[⟨T, W⟩]`. Any circuit that uses only one `K` or `K⁻¹` keeps every
matrix entry in `B + B K B`. Entries start in `B`. Gates `T` and `W` preserve
that bimodule, and so do ADD, SCALE, and BANK_SWAP. The single `K` or `K⁻¹`
sends a `B`-valued bank into `B K B`.

The output entry in row 3, input `a`, is exactly `-K W K T`. That monomial is
not in `⟨T, W⟩` and not in the double coset `⟨T, W⟩ K ⟨T, W⟩`, so it is outside
the bimodule. At least two `K`-family queries are required. They cost
`2 · 2 = 4`.

## One `W` query

Every word in `K` and `T` has identity address rows. The output contains
monomials whose address rows are `W`'s plane swap. At least one `W` or `W⁻¹`
is required. It costs 4.

## Total

The three families are disjoint paid natives, so the native mass is at least

```text
12 + 4 + 4 = 20.
```

The neutral gate list in `verify.py` (three `K`, six `T`, one `W`, together
with ADD, one rational scale, and one bank swap) reproduces the same matrix and
has mass 22. Mass 16 is impossible. Whether every circuit needs the third `K`,
which would raise the floor from 20 to 22, is open. Selected κ stays
`0.001672660197076918`.
