# Safe fusion of division-free dual-exchange consumers

For the exact PR6280 exchange compiler, a closed sequence of k same-pair
consumers needs only two exchanges, with scalar allowance **2B rather than
2kB**, while retaining every inner call. For its a=(3,5), b=(2,−1) example,
a closed three-call sequence has **128 rather than 384 scalar allowance**
(14 rather than 42 scalar gates), plus the same three inner calls and all
other delivery costs. **No new κ.**

This is a scoped algebraic composition result and independent source audit.
It supplies no actual P104 interval where fusion is legal. Cancellation of
inverse basis changes is classical; the contribution is the exact
source-bound contract, complete local bill and failure boundaries.

## Result and limits

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) proves the arbitrary-spare identity for any inner
function G, including nonlinear functions, and the ordered semigroup law
F_H∘F_G=F_(H∘G). It also proves that matching dual pairs are necessary and
sufficient for the entering/exiting exchanges to restore the arbitrary
spare and prevent its leakage into the data. These extend the predecessor's
one-use linear-operator statement; no error in that statement is asserted.

The fused internal representation differs from a returned consumer state.
An explicit observer distinguishes them. Fusion therefore requires a fixed
pair/chart/spare and no intermediate modification of the restored full-call
state. Read-only observations are permitted exactly when **O∘S=O** for the
universal all-inner-maps contract; a linear observer O(x,z)=Cx+Dz qualifies
exactly when D=CB. The proof distinguishes this universal condition from the
weaker condition on states reachable under one fixed nonsurjective call, and
retains every observation cost and physical access obligation. It does not supply exception-safe intermediate
return, rounded-error bounds, native scheduling, or new productive credit.

The independent symbolic certificate reduces only AB=I in a typed free
operator algebra. It permits no commutations. The actual source emitter is
then checked separately on exact coefficients; the author's dense verifier
and scientific suite are never imported. The nonlinear theorem rests on
direct substitution, while nonlinear executions are corroborating evidence.
