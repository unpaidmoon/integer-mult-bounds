# Full-column P104 wire entry and its exact cleanup query

The 11/19/487 one-helper meeting preserves the partner's original Y
column with **two ADDs**, after a precisely characterized entry change.
Original helper h cannot then be restored without new information.
Under field-linear readout, one pure-live cleanup query suffices exactly
when proportional to `d + lambda(c-e)`; its minimum support is **eight**.
For dyadic scalar operations its scale must also be a dyadic unit
`+/-2^k`: `q=3d` fails, while the sharp attainer `q=d` is legal. A public query with
no owned-delivered live component needs a frame of dimension at least
**six**. **No new κ.**

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives the quantified entry theorem, exact construction,
cleanup classification, named source contact, partial paid ledger and limits.
This is a local interface theorem, not a complete chronological one-helper program.

[PR6261](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6261) explicitly
supplied a response projection. Adding the original partner column to its
literal entry produces an unwanted `-Y` in the outgoing wire. The corrected
entry is `(Y+2eX, (a-e)X-h, dX+h)`; two ADDs produce
`(Y+(c+e)X, (c-e)X, dX+h)`. This resolves the full-column meeting boundary
without contradicting the predecessor's stated projected theorem.

The minimum cleanup query is `dX`; its preparation is not free. A conditional
source-loaded calendar uses four visible ADDs and incoming ranks `{1:4,6:1}`.
Preparation of the dirty carriers and clean query, outgoing delivery, and
last-wire restoration remain additional. The singleton stop already increases
the partner's concave paid moment. No favorable complete invoice is claimed.
